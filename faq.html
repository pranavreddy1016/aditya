Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Knowledge Hub

🌾 Farmer to Customer Marketplace

How do farmers sell directly to customers on this platform?
Farmers can list their fresh produce directly through our mobile app or web portal. Customers browse the local listings, place orders, and pay securely online. The platform bypasses traditional middlemen to ensure better profits for farmers and fresher goods for buyers.
How is the pricing of agricultural products determined?
Farmers retain full control over their pricing. However, our platform provides real-time data from local APMC markets (Mandis) to help farmers set fair, competitive, and profitable prices.
What measures ensure the quality of produce delivered to customers?
We implement a strict quality-check protocol at the point of collection. Produce is graded and packed securely to prevent bruising or damage during transit, ensuring farm-fresh delivery.

🚚 Drivers & Logistics Network

How can truck or commercial vehicle drivers partner with us?
Drivers can register under the "Logistics Partner" section by uploading their commercial driving license, vehicle registration (RC), and identity proof. Once verified, they can accept delivery jobs through the driver app.
How are delivery routes and payouts calculated for drivers?
Routes are optimized automatically using GPS to save fuel and time. Payouts are calculated based on total distance, vehicle type, and weight of the load, paid out weekly directly to the driver's bank account.
What happens if there is a delay or issue during transit?
Drivers have access to 24/7 dedicated support via the app. In case of breakdowns, our backend team coordinates with nearby emergency logistics partners to transfer the perishable load promptly.

📖 Knowledge About Agricultural India

What are the main crop seasons in India?
India has three primary cropping seasons: Kharif (Sown in June-July with monsoons, e.g., Rice, Maize), Rabi (Sown in Oct-Nov during winter, e.g., Wheat, Mustard), and Zaid (Short summer season, e.g., Watermelon, Cucumber).
How does this project support sustainable and organic farming in India?
Our portal hosts educational resources, government scheme updates (like PM-KISAN), and expert advice on organic fertilizers, crop rotation, and water conservation technologies to empower Indian farmers.
